FAQs
How does NiteLab Pro work?
It gently positions your jaw to reduce airway blockage, helping to prevent snoring.
The adjustable settings let you customize the fit for the most effective and comfortable experience.
Is this a treatment for Sleep Apnea?
NiteLab is not intended to treat sleep apnea. It’s designed to reduce snoring in adults. If you have symptoms of sleep apnea, we recommend speaking with a healthcare professional to determine the best solution for you.
Is it comfortable to wear all night?
Experts and testers say a solid YES. The ultra-thin design makes it easy to wear, and the flexible material adapts to your mouth’s natural shape. 
It’s also side-sleeper friendly and stays in place throughout the night.
How do I adjust the settings for the best fit?
NiteLab Pro has 25 precision adjustment settings to fine-tune your jaw position. Simply detach and reattach the trays in the position you prefer.
You can follow the included guide or watch our video tutorial for step-by-step instructions.
Is it safe to use?
Absolutely! NiteLab Pro is made from premium medical-grade, BPA-free materials, ensuring a safe and durable anti-snoring solution.
Does NiteLab Pro work for all mouth sizes?
Yes! With Adaptive Fit Technology, it flexes to fit your mouth comfortably, regardless of size or shape.
What if it doesn’t work for me?
We stand by our Quiet Nights Guarantee—try it risk-free for 90 nights. If you’re not satisfied, return it for a full refund. 

No hassle, no stress—just better sleep, guaranteed.
When will I receive my order?
We ship all our orders within 24-48 hours of receipt. 

You will be notified by email and given a tracking number that you can use to track your delivery on both our website and the courier's website.
Contraindications of use
Do not use it if you have central sleep apnea, severe respiratory disorder, asthma, loose teeth, or advanced periodontal disease, temporomandibular disorder, have a dental implant less than 1 year old, or are under the age of 18.

Use of this device may cause tooth movement or changes in dental occlusion (bite pattern), tooth or gum soreness, pain or soreness of the jaw, obstruction of oral breathing, and excessive salivation.

This product is not for the prevention of bruxism or teeth grinding.
